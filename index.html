FAQ.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

Yes. That message shows for new apps from small developers that aren't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. Catalyst runs only on your PC and doesn't need an account. Every update is signed, so Catalyst only installs updates that really came from us.

How do I unlock Pro after buying?

After you pay, you land on a page with your key (it starts with CAT1-). In Catalyst, go to Settings › Get Pro › Enter key, paste it and press Unlock. Bookmark the key page to see your key again later.

Can I use Pro on more than one PC?

Yes, one key works on up to 3 PCs. To move it, go to Settings › Pro › Remove key on the old PC first.

Is it a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase, and updates are free.

Is there a Mac version?

A Mac beta is in testing. The Windows version comes first; the Mac version will land here when it's ready.

What does Catalyst do with my data?

Nothing leaves your PC unless you turn on a feature that goes online, like weather or update checks. There's no account and no tracking server. You can pause time tracking, export or delete everything in Settings › Privacy. See the privacy policy.

Refunds and support

Payments are handled by Stripe. For refunds or payment questions, reply to your receipt email. See the terms.
