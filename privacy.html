Privacy.

Last updated October 2026

Short version

Catalyst runs on your PC. There's no account and no Catalyst server collecting your data. Your settings, library and stats are stored only on your PC.

What stays on your PC

  • Your apps, games, pins, workspaces and settings
  • Time tracking (which app is in front), if you leave it on
  • Clipboard history, kept in memory only and cleared when Catalyst closes
  • The file-name index for search (names only, never file contents)

You can pause tracking, turn features off, export a copy or delete everything in Settings › Privacy.

When Catalyst goes online

  • Update checks: asks GitHub whether a new version exists, and checks the update's signature before installing it.
  • Pro activation: sends your key and an anonymous PC ID (a scrambled code, not your name) to our key server once per PC, so a key can be limited to 3 PCs.
  • Features you turn on: weather (sends your city name), Roblox player counts, creator stats (reads your own public Spotify, YouTube, TikTok or SoundCloud page), site icons (asks each site you saved for its own icon), Steam friends (your own API key), Discord status (local only), phone remote (your home network only), sync (your own cloud folder).

Purchases

When you buy Pro, Stripe processes your payment and receives your email and payment details under its own privacy policy. Our key server reads only whether the checkout was paid and the email it was paid with, to make your key.

Contact

Reply to your Stripe receipt email with any privacy questions.
